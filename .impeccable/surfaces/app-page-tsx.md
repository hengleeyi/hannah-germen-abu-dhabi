# Home (one-page site)

Scope: the whole one-page website at `/`. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: adult expats (career, move to DACH, Goethe exams) and parents of children, equal weight.
Action: book a free online trial through the booking calendar, or email Hannah.
Proof: only Hannah's own facts from PRODUCT.md. No testimonials, counts or ratings.
Constraints: no German flag colours (no black, red or gold anywhere), not corporate, not childish, not plain, no German cliché. Strong SEO: one h1 with "German lessons in Abu Dhabi", JSON-LD, sitemap, robots, OG image.
Open: email, booking URL, prices, domain, duration of lessons, "by weekly" writing cadence (weekly or every two weeks).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page speaks in the article colour code every German learner memorises: der blue, die rose, das green. Every section is a German noun, and its article decides its colour. It refuses the tutor template of portrait-beside-headline on white with benefit cards.

OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed fields of der blue (#2f5fd0), die rose (#e86fa5) and das green (deepened for contrast), indigo ink (#1b2547) and white. Hard edges, no gradients, no shadows. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Atkinson Hyperlegible for body. Every section header is a lockup: article huge, noun beside it, English gloss small.

STORY: The visitor learns the colour code in seconds, sees Hannah as die Lehrerin, picks der Beruf (adults) or das Kind (children), sees the method, the levels and the prices, and books die Probestunde online or writes to her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Three vertical fields, widths about 46/27/27. der Unterricht (blue) holds nav and the h1. die Lehrerin (rose) holds Hannah's portrait and one line on her. das Ziel (green) holds the two doors and the primary action "Book a free online trial" plus "Email Hannah". On phones the fields stack as bands, action inside the first screen.
