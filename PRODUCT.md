## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (user's choice). One-page website. Strong SEO is a stated requirement: server-rendered HTML, complete metadata, structured data (LocalBusiness / Person / Course), sitemap and robots.

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight, confirmed by the user:

- Adult learners: international expats in Abu Dhabi and the UAE at different career stages. Many want German to work, build a career or move to Germany, Austria, Switzerland or the Netherlands. Some prepare for Goethe-Institut exams, up to C1 and C2.
- Parents of children and young adults in Abu Dhabi who look for a German tutor for their child. Hannah mostly teaches children and young adults, but teaches any age.

Their job on the site: decide whether Hannah is the right teacher, understand the format (in person or online) and the price, then contact her or book a first lesson.

## Product Purpose

A personal one-page website for Hannah, a private German teacher in Abu Dhabi. It replaces an informal social post ("PM me for more info") with a professional presence that ranks in search for queries such as "German teacher Abu Dhabi", "German lessons Abu Dhabi", "learn German online" and "Goethe exam preparation Abu Dhabi". Success means qualified enquiries by email and bookings through the calendar.

## Positioning

- Native German speaker who grew up, lived and worked in Austria.
- Over 10 years of teaching German as a foreign language.
- Also worked for several years as a designer and in product management in Austria, Germany, Switzerland and the Netherlands. Now a Product Designer in the UAE. Students get first-hand insight into the job market and daily life in those four countries, which a generic tutor cannot offer.
- Speaks German, English, Dutch and Japanese.
- Bachelor's degree in Product Design from the Netherlands.

## Operating Context

- Lessons happen in person in Abu Dhabi or online, whichever the learner prefers.
- Each course starts with a level assessment and a talk about personal and professional goals. Hannah then builds a tailored learning plan, adapted to the learner's pace and learning style.
- Method: conversational practice plus grammar plus biweekly writing sessions. The source text says "equal parts grammar and writing practice". Courses also cover reading, listening comprehension and pronunciation.
- Exam preparation for Goethe-Institut exams.
- Learners can reach out between lessons with questions or when stuck on an exercise.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact channels: email and a booking calendar (for example Calendly). No WhatsApp, no social links unless the user adds them later.
- Free trial lesson: offered for online lessons only. In-person lessons have no free trial. The site must state this condition clearly.
- Prices: the user will provide real prices per lesson. Until then, the price section uses clearly marked placeholders that must not ship.
- Open, not yet provided: email address, booking calendar URL, price values and currency, final domain (needed for canonical URL, sitemap and structured data), Hannah's surname (if she wants it on the site).
- Site language: English. German phrases may appear as accents. Other languages are undecided.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion. Every claim on the page comes from Hannah's own text. Real experience carries the page, not invented proof.
2. Two doors, one teacher. Adults and parents both find their path quickly, without a second page.
3. Personal, not corporate. The page is one person's practice. It speaks in her voice and shows her face.
4. Every section earns search value. Copy names the place (Abu Dhabi, UAE), the format (in person, online) and the outcomes (Goethe exams, A1 to C2, career) in natural language.
5. One clear next step. Each visitor ends with email or the booking calendar, and the free online trial is stated with its condition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience is international and multilingual, often non-native English readers. Use plain English. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
